Learn the basics

Four short ideas explain everything you can do in this simulator.

Buying (going long)

When you buy a share you own a small piece of the company. If the price goes up you can sell it for more than you paid. If the price goes down you lose the difference. The most you can lose is the money you paid.

Selling

Selling closes a position you own. The gain or loss becomes “realized” at that moment. Until then it is only an “unrealized” number that changes with the price.

Short selling

A short seller borrows shares, sells them now, and hopes to buy them back later at a lower price. The difference is the profit. In this simulator, Short opens that bet and Cover closes it by buying the shares back.

Why short selling is riskier

A share can fall only to zero, so a short seller’s gain is limited. A share can rise without limit, so a short seller’s loss is not limited. In Vietnam, short selling by individual investors is restricted, so this page is for learning only. Check the current rules and talk to a licensed securities broker before trading real money.

Quick answers

Are the prices real?

No. Prices are generated randomly in your browser every two seconds. They show how a price can move, not what FPT shares are really worth.

What is a lot?

A lot is a standard order size. Here every order must be a multiple of 100 shares.

Where is my account saved?

In your own browser, using local storage. It stays on your device and you can reset it on the Portfolio page.

What are P&L and equity?

P&L means profit and loss. Equity is your cash plus the value of shares you own, minus the value of shares you owe back.
